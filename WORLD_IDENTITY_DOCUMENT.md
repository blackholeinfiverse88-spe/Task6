
## 1. Purpose

This document defines the core identity of the game world and ensures:

- All characters operate within a unified philosophical framework.
- Karma influences are consistent across systems.
- Battlefield behavior aligns with intended experiential meaning.
- No contradiction exists between world logic and match engine logic.

This document acts as the identity anchor for battlefield validation and engine safety.

---

## 2. Core World Philosophy

The world operates on a Karma-driven balance system.

Karma is not cosmetic.
Karma directly influences:

- Behavioral amplification
- Combat intensity
- Stability vs volatility
- Player perception

The world is designed around dynamic moral equilibrium rather than static alignment.

---

## 3. Karma States Definition

### Good Karma

World Condition:
- Stabilized energy flow
- Predictable combat patterns
- Defensive or supportive bias

Gameplay Impact:
- Reduced volatility
- Controlled aggression
- Tactical clarity

Player Feeling:
- Safety
- Order
- Moral clarity

---

### Neutral Karma

World Condition:
- Balanced but adaptive environment
- No amplification bias

Gameplay Impact:
- Standard stat behavior
- Baseline combat interaction
- No emotional exaggeration

Player Feeling:
- Tactical focus
- Strategic neutrality

---

### Bad Karma

World Condition:
- Intensified battlefield volatility
- Increased unpredictability
- Heightened aggression

Gameplay Impact:
- Higher risk–reward patterns
- Faster engagement cycles
- Amplified offensive behavior

Player Feeling:
- Pressure
- Urgency
- Moral tension

- Reflect the current Karma state

Authority remains with the Match Engine.
